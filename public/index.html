Resilience & Circuit Breakers

3-Layer Protection

1
Provider Circuit Breaker

Trips on 408/5xx errors. Threshold: 5 failures. Cooldown: 60s with HALF-OPEN probe.

2
Connection Cooldown

Exponential backoff with anti-thundering-herd guard. 429 honors Retry-After.

3
Model Lockout

Per-model 429/404 lockout. Only locks the failing model, never the whole provider.
